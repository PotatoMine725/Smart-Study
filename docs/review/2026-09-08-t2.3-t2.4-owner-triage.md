## 0. Verdict (read this first)

### **NOT READY — OWNER TRIAGE REQUIRED**

(Review-folder vocabulary: **`block`** for anything that touches persistence; the T2.3 pure-merge
core can be unblocked by three small rulings — see §9.)

**Why, in five lines.**

1. **D5-D / D6-G / D8-B ("unresolved conflict ⇒ live DB = Base") cannot be implemented
   mechanically without a policy choice.** On the device that authored a candidate, that candidate
   is *already live*. Keeping live = Base means un-materialising the device's own row. Measured on
   this tree: `UNIQUE(TaskNotes.MaTask)` also covers **tombstoned** rows, `Remove()` is converted
   to a tombstone by `SyncStamper`, and the Epic-1 invariant forbids hard deletes. Every viable
   mechanism (audited hard delete · partial unique index + resurrection semantics · amend D5-D)
   changes something the owner froze. → **D9-T1**.
2. **D3 does not define a winner for a full tie with *different* values** (equal `ModifiedAtUtc`,
   equal `ModifiedByDeviceId`). The tie is reachable: `DeviceIdentity` seeds the persisted id from
   the hostname by design and documents the collision. → **D9-T2**.
3. **The per-entity merge surface (OPEN-7) is unratified, and the two prior planning documents
   contradict each other on `StudyLog.DeviceId`.** → **D9-T3**.
4. **Two live defects sit directly under the T2.4 apply path** and must be fixed in separate PRs
   before persistence work: (a) any DB upgraded from pre-Epic-1 **rejects every EF insert into
   `TaskNotes`** (`NOT NULL constraint failed: TaskNotes.UpdatedAtUtc`, measured on the dev DB copy
   through the real context); (b) `TaskReferenceLink` edits reset `Rev` to 1 **and** rewrite
   `CreatedAtUtc` on every save (OPEN-8, confirmed live). → **D9-E6 / D9-E7**.
5. Everything else in D1–D9 is either **implementable as-is** or **implementable with engineering
   design that needs no owner policy** (seam, Rev-on-apply, DateTime `Kind`, canonical
   serialisation, transaction boundary, drift check). None of it contradicts current code.

**Smallest safe slice after triage:** the T2.3 pure merge core (DTOs, merge-surface registry,
versioned `SnapshotJson` serialiser, three-way per-field merge with LWW, `ConflictCandidate`
output for all three conflict kinds) with fixture tests — **no persistence, no seam** — gated only
on D9-T2 and D9-T3 (and the OPEN-5 engineering rule). T2.4 persistence follows once D9-T1,
D9-T4/T5/T6 and the two defect PRs land.

## 13. Final notes

The frozen semantic design is sound and mostly implementable; the codebase does not contradict its
merge semantics. What it contradicts is the *staging* semantics for unresolved constraint and
structural conflicts, because the record never had to say how a device withdraws its own live row
under a unique index that counts tombstones and an invariant that forbids deletes. That one ruling
(D9-T1), two small tie/surface rulings (D9-T2/T3), and two defect PRs (E6/E7) separate this tree
from a clean T2.3 start. The engineering seam (E1) is well-bounded and needs no owner policy.
