## 10. Final notes

D-5 is implemented cleanly and narrowly: one try/catch around the whole transaction, a decision-based fence exclusion, a membership-only
routine with a collection-swap undo, and a repair whose own failure cannot hide the real one. My probes found no data loss, no
duplicate and no tombstone on any failure point, including reparent and cancellation cases the PR's tests do not stage. What I
found is at the edges of the claims: a status row, a comment, a guarantee with no test, and a stamp that the test double cannot
produce. The one structural lesson is the last: `FailingSaveDbContext` throws before `SyncStamper` runs, so "failed save" tests
that use it describe a cleaner post-failure graph than a real provider failure leaves.

**What I could not run.** A second OS process holding the SQLite write lock (the addendum accepts the interceptor). The WPF
handler line in `App.xaml.cs` (no harness; accepted). Mutants M2 and M4-M8 (report-only). GitNexus (stale); scope was checked by
`git diff` and grep, and the only caller of `RepairFailureOf` in production is `App.xaml.cs:43`.
